where a u home ("we") operates Monipet (모니펫), a desktop pet game for Windows and Android (the "Service"), in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the Service processes and why, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Summary
  • Both the PC app and the phone app start with Google or Kakao sign-in (there is no way to start without signing in). When you sign in we receive that account's email address, and you choose a nickname that your friends will see.
  • Sign-in information is only used to protect your pet, move it to another device or recover it, verify your identity and answer your inquiries. Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Only users aged 14 or older can use the Service. We do not collect personal information from children under 14 (section 6).
  • The Android app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads, only when you tap "watch an ad" yourself. There are no banner or interstitial ads. While an ad is shown, Google processes your advertising ID and related data (section 3, Ads). The Windows app has no ads.
  • No other analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. No access to location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • We never sell your data.
  • Paid purchases are not offered at this time.
  • You can delete your account and all game data in the app at any time.

What other players can see

Only players you have become friends with by exchanging friend codes (and players who share a friend boss code with you) can see your nickname (with the last 4 characters of your friend code), your pet's name, appearance (species, cosmetics) and level, and greetings and hearts during visits and at the playground. Strangers cannot see them, and there is no free-text chat. Your email address and Google or Kakao profile (name, picture) are never shown to other players.

2. Retention

  • Your account and game data are kept until you delete your account, and are erased immediately when you do.
  • Internal accounts with no sign-in method attached (for example, an app closed before sign-in finished, or an unlinked phone) and no pets are deleted automatically after 30 days without use.
  • Your nickname and nickname reports are kept until the account is deleted. If either the reporting or the reported account is deleted, the report is deleted with it.
  • Pairing codes expire after 10 minutes; pairing attempt counts reset every 10 minutes.
  • The age check record is kept until the account is deleted.
  • To-dos are kept until the account is deleted and are deleted together with it. A to-do you delete is kept only as a deletion mark for 30 days (so your other devices stay in sync), and "today only" to-dos are deleted 7 days after their day. To-do titles are never shown to other users.
  • Ad reward records are kept until the account is deleted; ad verification diagnostic logs (no account data) are deleted after 7 days. Data Google processes to show ads is kept under Google's policies.
  • Server access logs are kept for a short period under our hosting provider's (Supabase) policy and then deleted.
  • Where the law requires longer retention, data is kept only for that period.

Ads (rewarded ads in the Android app)

  • The Android app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. An ad appears only when you tap a "watch an ad" button yourself, and watching it to the end gives an in-game reward (such as faster egg hatching, extra expedition coins or a free egg draw). There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads, and you can play without watching ads. The Windows app has no ads.
  • While loading and showing an ad, Google may collect and process your advertising ID (Android advertising ID), device and app information (device type, OS version, app version, etc.), approximate location inferred from your IP address, and your interactions with the ad, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services (policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
  • Where required, Google's consent form appears the first time you watch an ad; if you do not consent, non-personalized ads are shown. Ads are limited to content rated T (teen) or below.
  • When you finish an ad, Google sends a verification to our server (including your account ID, reward slot, one-time number and ad transaction ID); the server checks Google's signature and then grants the reward. We do not link this information to your advertising ID.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on your Android device, open Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices Settings → Privacy → Ads) and tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID". You can still watch rewarded ads afterwards, but they will be non-personalized.

4. How to delete your account and data

In the app (immediate)

  • Android: ⚙️ Settings (설정) at the top right of the screen → 👤 Manage account (계정 관리) → 🗑 Delete account (계정 삭제) → read the notice and tap Continue (계속) → type "삭제" ("delete") → Confirm with Google (or Kakao) and delete permanently (구글로 확인하고 영구 삭제 — you sign in once more to verify it is you)
  • Windows: Monipet icon in the taskbar notification area (system tray) → 📖 Open Pet Notebook (펫 수첩 열기) → ⚙️ Settings (설정) at the top right → 👤 Account (계정) → 🗑 Delete account (계정 삭제) → read the notice and click Continue (계속) → type "삭제" ("delete") → Delete permanently (영구 삭제)

Deletion immediately and permanently erases from our servers that account's pet save, owned pets, in-game currency, cosmetics, all logs (including ad reward records), friend, visit and playground records, device pairing data, nickname, and linked sign-in methods (Google, Kakao). It cannot be undone. Deleting from the phone also deletes the linked PC pet account. Deleting from the PC also unlinks any paired phones. Signing in again with the same Google or Kakao account will not bring the pet back. No data is retained afterwards (the hosting provider's short-lived security logs are deleted automatically as described in section 2).

If you cannot use the app (email request)

Email cameo.moment@gmail.com with the subject "Monipet account deletion request" and the email address linked to your account (for Google or Kakao sign-in, that account's email) or your friend code. After verifying your identity through the linked email, we will delete the account within 7 days and let you know. A phone linked only with a PC code has no sign-in information of its own, so please send the email of the account you signed in with on the PC.

5. Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your information, in the app or by emailing cameo.moment@gmail.com. We will handle it without delay.

6. Children and teens

  • Children under 14 cannot use the Service, and we do not collect personal information from children under 14. The sign-in screen of the phone and PC apps has a checkbox "I am 14 or older and agree to the privacy policy", and you must tick it to sign in or link an account. We do not ask for birth year, month or day; only a record that you ticked it and signed in is kept on the account.
  • If we learn that a child under 14 ticked the box and used the Service, we will delete that account's information. A parent or guardian can request deletion by email (section 4).
  • Paid purchases are not offered at this time. If paid features are added, we will explain them in this policy and in the app before any payment.

7. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every database table has row-level access control: you can read only your own account's data (and that of your paired devices), and all writes go through server functions that check account permissions.
  • Sign-in uses standard OAuth (PKCE); we never receive your Google or Kakao password.
